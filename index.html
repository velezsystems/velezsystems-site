About VAS

Velez Advanced Systems (VAS) is a research driven robotics and applied artificial intelligence company that provides engineering services and agentic AI analysis systems in support of technical evaluation, image based assessment, and decision support activities.

Our phased prototyping approach supports both internal development and client facing engineering services, ensuring systems and analytical capabilities are validated prior to deployment.

Engineering & Agentic AI Services

Velez Advanced Systems provides professional engineering services and agentic AI based analysis, including technical assessment, image driven evaluation, and AI-assisted decision support. Services and systems are delivered through professional engagements and controlled software deployments.

Current Project

As part of its engineering services and agentic AI system development activities, Velez Advanced Systems is currently in Phase 1 of a humanoid robotics program. This 1:3 scale prototype focuses on gait, balance, and servo control. It integrates:

  • Dynamixel smart servos
  • Solid-state Li-ion battery systems
  • 9-DoF IMUs for orientation and balance
  • Custom spine and joint assembly for realistic motion
